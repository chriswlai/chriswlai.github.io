Christopher Lai

I am a first-year Master's student in Robotics at the University of Pennsylvania, currently working with Professor Rahul Mangharam in xLab (Safe Autonomous Systems Lab). Previously, I received my B.S. in Computer Engineering from UC Santa Barbara, where I conducted research in computer vision and multimodal learning in the Computer Vision Research Lab led by Yuan-Fang Wang, and the Four Eyes Lab under Tobias Höllerer.

I'm always happy to connect about computer vision, robotics, and interesting engineering opportunities.

Technical Interests

I'm interested in computer vision, multimodal perception, and learning-based autonomy, building systems that understand dynamic environments and support reliable decisions. My interests span geometric vision and scene understanding, visual tracking and motion prediction, and relational and temporal learning for multi-agent interactions. I'm also interested in the robustness, observability, and evaluation of perception systems, particularly as they connect to decision-making in robotics and autonomous driving.
